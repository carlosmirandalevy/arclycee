ArcLycée

Dominican Archaeological Adventure

A 2D educational RPG to explore the archaeological heritage of the Dominican Republic. Created by les fous du robot from the Robotics class at the Liceo Francés de Santo Domingo — 2026.

ES EN FR
Play Documentation Archpedia

13 Worlds

From Cuevas del Pomier to the Museo del Hombre Dominicano. Each world teaches an aspect of Dominican heritage.

Pacifist Route

Inspired by Undertale: every conflict can be resolved through dialogue, negotiation and citizen activism.

3 Languages

Fully trilingual: Spanish, English and French. Switch at any time from the menu.

Pedagogical

600 assessment questions, educator guide, complete encyclopedia. Designed for the classroom.

Student-Made

Les fous du robot — Robotics class at the Liceo Francés de Santo Domingo, 2026.

Open Source

HTML5 Canvas + vanilla JavaScript. Code documented in Spanish, readable by 13-year-old students.
