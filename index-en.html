A super fun, engaging adventure videogame to explore the archaeological heritage of the Dominican Republic. Created by Les Fous du Robot students from the Robotics class at the Liceo Francés de Santo Domingo — 2026.

13 Worlds

From Cuevas del Pomier to the Museo del Hombre Dominicano. Each world teaches an aspect of Dominican heritage.

Pacifist Route
