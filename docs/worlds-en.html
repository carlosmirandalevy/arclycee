🌍 Worlds and Levels

Each world is based on a real location in the Dominican Republic.

🕳️ Pomier Caves
2D Platformer

The Pomier Caves (San Cristóbal) are the largest cave art system in the Caribbean, with over 6,000 Taíno petroglyphs. This is the first level: a dark cave where you discover the Taíno heritage while dodging dangers.

Mechanics

  • Darkness system: 80px light radius that expands with the flashlight
  • 5 petroglyphs: sun, bat, face, spiral, frog — each with educational dialogue
  • 3 vandal graffiti: show the real problem of cave damage
  • Mossy platforms, water drips, dust particles, depth parallax

NPCs

🔬
Dr. Martínez
Archaeologist

Appears near the exit. Explains that the Taínos adorned their cemís with guanín — an alloy of gold, silver, and copper — especially on the eyes and sensory organs. Gives you Magnoboot, your first robot companion.

Items

🔦 Flashlight

Expands your light radius in the darkness.

🗺️ Map Fragment

Parchment with clues about the island.

🗿 Taíno Artifact

A cemí with golden guanín details on its eyes and sensory organs.

🏛️ Zona Colonial of Santo Domingo
Top-Down UNESCO Heritage

The Zona Colonial of Santo Domingo, declared a World Heritage Site by UNESCO in 1990. Contains the first permanent European constructions in the Americas. Here you face Constructor Méndez with civic activism tools.

Historical Monuments

⛪ Cathedral Primada of America

First cathedral of the New World (1512-1540). Isabelline Gothic style with rose window and twin towers.

🏛️ Cathedral Museum

15 halls in the restored Real Cárcel. 16th-20th century treasures: Columbus' portapaz, Bicephalous Eagle, Lower Choir.

🏥 San Nicolás de Bari Hospital

First hospital in the Americas (1503). Ruins with round arches.

🏚️ San Francisco Monastery

First monastery in the Americas (1508). Cloister with columns, now ruins for concerts.

🇩🇴 National Pantheon

Neoclassical facade with columns and eternal flame. Guards from the First Regiment.

🛤️ Calle de las Damas

First paved street in the Americas (1502). Colonial lanterns with flickering light.

☀️ Sundial

One of the oldest in the Americas (16th century). Slowly rotating gnomon.

🌊 Aquatic World — La Pinta Shipwreck
Top-Down

Returning to La Isabela, a fisherman offers Pepito a ride in his canoe. He takes him over the shipwreck of La Pinta (whose timbers were used to build Fort Navidad).

Marine Wildlife

🐢 Sea Turtles (4 species)

Hawksbill (critically endangered), Leatherback (largest), Green (herbivore), Loggerhead (shell-crusher).

🐋 Humpback Whales

Migrate from December to March to the north coast to mate.

🐬 Bottlenose Dolphins

Common Caribbean species. Seen year-round.

🦭 Antillean Manatee

Pre-Columbian species endangered.

🐠 Parrotfish

Vital for reefs. Produces the white sand of beaches.

Threats and Enemies

🦁 Lionfish

Invasive species. Combat encounter with 4 ecological options.

🪼 Jellyfish

Passive obstacle: damage + slowness effect.

Characters

🎣
Fisherman Manuel
Story of La Pinta

Tells how La Pinta was dismantled to build Fort Navidad.

🐢
Hawksbill Turtle
Endangered species education

Swims in an oval circuit. Teaches about its conservation status.

🤿
Underwater Archaeologist
Delivers shipwreck map

Documents the island's 400+ shipwrecks.

Implemented Mechanics

  • Underwater movement (speed x 0.7)
  • 4 jellyfish as passive hazards (damage + slowness)
  • Ecological combat against lionfish
  • Hawksbill turtle in oval circuit
  • 25 bubble particles + 20 seaweed + 4 light rays
  • Depth sorting by Y

🤖 LFSD — Side Quests

The robotics class at the Lycée Français de Saint-Domingue offers 3 side quests with unique mini-games.

🔧 LFSD Robotics Class

An indoor level with 8 student NPCs from "les fous du robot". Three quest-givers offer mini-games.

Side Quests

  • Good Vibrations (Émile) — Calibrate the magnetometer by adjusting frequency, amplitude, and phase to match a target wave in 45 seconds.
    Discovered in La Isabela, played and completed in LFSD (+15 reputation).
  • Full Metal Archeologist (Sofía) — Program an underwater robot with instruction blocks.
    Discovered in the Manatee Sanctuary, played in LFSD, completed by delivering the robot (+15 LFSD rep., +10 delivery rep.).
  • Weird Science (Lucas) — Connect 6 colored cables in 30 seconds.
    Discovered in the Museum, played in LFSD (+15 rep., +5 bonus if under 15s), completed by delivering the equipment (+10 rep.).
