📖 The Story

An adventure spanning 5000 years of Dominican history.

You play as Pepito or Pepita, a 14-year-old with Taíno, Spanish and African ancestry. One day, while walking through a construction site in Santo Domingo, you discover a mysterious relic glowing with a strange light. When you touch it, the ground gives way beneath your feet and you fall into the Cuevas del Pomier — a real cave system with thousands of Taíno petroglyphs.

Thus begins your adventure as an accidental archaeologist. With the help of three companions — a digging robot, a stray dog and a cemí spirit — you will travel through worlds spanning from Taíno settlements to the Colonial Zone of Santo Domingo, facing conflicts between modern development and heritage preservation.

Not everything is solved by fighting. Inspired by Undertale, the game offers a full pacifist route: you can convince your adversaries through dialogue, negotiation and civic activism. Every conflict has a solution that does not require violence.

🗺️ Game Flow

Free-exploration map with 12 levels, Super Mario World style. The player moves freely with a sprite across the map and activates nodes by approaching them.

Side Quests

7 optional quests discovered during the main story:

  • Batú — Taíno ball game with Guarocuya.
  • Manatee Rescue — Free a manatee + clean reef.
  • Good Vibrations — Calibrate magnetometer (LFSD).
  • Full Metal Archeologist — Program submarine robot (LFSD).
  • Weird Science — Connect cables (LFSD).
  • Cathedral Museum — Sacred artifact → Fabiola Herrera.
  • The Idol of Enriquillo — Sacred cemí → Enriquillo.
