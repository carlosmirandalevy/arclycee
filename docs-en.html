📖 The Story

An adventure through 5,000 years of Dominican history.

You play as Pepito or Pepita, a 14-year-old teenager with Taíno, Spanish, and African ancestry. One day, while walking through a construction site in Santo Domingo, you discover a mysterious relic glowing with a strange light. When you touch it, the ground gives way beneath your feet and you fall into the Cuevas del Pomier — a real cave system with thousands of Taíno petroglyphs.

Thus begins your adventure as an accidental archaeologist. With the help of three companions — a digging robot, a stray dog, and a cemí spirit — you will travel through worlds spanning from Taíno settlements to the Zona Colonial of Santo Domingo, facing conflicts between modern development and heritage preservation.

Not everything is solved by fighting. Inspired by Undertale, the game offers a full pacifist route: you can convince your adversaries through dialogue, negotiation, and civic activism. Every conflict has a solution that does not require violence.

🌍 Worlds and Levels

Each world is based on a real location in the Dominican Republic.

🕳️ Cuevas del Pomier
2D Platformer

The Cuevas del Pomier (San Cristóbal) are the largest rock art system in the Caribbean, with over 6,000 Taíno petroglyphs. It is the first level of the game: a dark cave where you discover Taíno heritage while dodging hazards.

Cuevas del Pomier — 3000×540px — Platformer with darkness

Mechanics

  • Darkness system: 80px light radius that expands with the flashlight
  • 5 petroglyphs: sun, bat, face, spiral, frog — each with educational dialogue
  • 3 vandal graffiti: showcase the real problem of cave damage
  • Mossy platforms, water drips, dust particles, depth parallax

🏛️ Zona Colonial de Santo Domingo
Top-Down UNESCO Heritage

The Zona Colonial of Santo Domingo, declared a World Heritage Site by UNESCO in 1990. It contains the first permanent European constructions in the Americas. Here you face Constructor Méndez with civic activism tools.

Zona Colonial — 1800×1200px — UNESCO Heritage

Historical Monuments

⛪ Catedral Primada de América

First cathedral of the New World (1512-1540). Isabelline Gothic style with rose window and twin towers.

🏛️ Cathedral Museum

15 rooms in the restored Royal Jail. Treasures from the 16th-20th centuries: Columbus's pax, Bicephalous Eagle, Lower Choir.

🏥 Hospital San Nicolás de Bari

First hospital in the Americas (1503). Ruins with semicircular arches.

🏚️ San Francisco Monastery

First monastery in the Americas (1508). Cloister with columns, now ruins used for concerts.

🇩🇴 National Pantheon

Neoclassical facade with columns and eternal flame. First Regiment guards.

🛤️ Calle de las Damas

First cobblestone street in the Americas (1502). Colonial lanterns with flickering light.

☀️ Sundial

One of the oldest in the Americas (16th c.). Gnomon that slowly rotates.

Marine Wildlife

The aquatic world features the real biodiversity of the Dominican Caribbean:

🐢 Sea Turtles (4 species)

Carey (Hawksbill) — critically endangered, nests on Dominican beaches.
Tinglar (Leatherback) — the world's largest, up to 2 meters.
Green Turtle — herbivore, maintains seagrass meadows.
Caguama (Loggerhead) — large-headed, crushes shells with its jaw.

🐋 Humpback Whales

Migrate from December to March to Hispaniola's north coast to mate and give birth. Samaná Bay is one of the world's best spots to observe them.

🐬 Bottlenose Dolphins

Common Caribbean species. Intelligent and sociable, seen off Dominican coasts year-round.

🦭 Antillean Manatee

Pre-Columbian endangered species. The Taínos knew and depicted it in their art. Lives in shallow coastal waters and estuaries.

🐠 Parrotfish

Vital for coral reef health: eats algae that smother coral and produces white beach sand by digesting coral fragments.

⚔️ Combat System

Inspired by Undertale: you can fight OR convince the enemy to calm down.

Base Mechanics

Each enemy has two emotional meters:

  • Convincing (0-100): rises when talking or negotiating. When it reaches 100, the enemy is convinced and the conflict resolves peacefully. The label changes per enemy: "Convinced" for people, "Controlled" for the lionfish.
  • Hostility (0-100): drops with diplomacy. When below 30, the enemy may talk instead of attacking.

Ecological Combat: Lionfish

In the Aquatic World, the combat against the lionfish uses ecological control options. The lionfish is a real invasive species from the Indo-Pacific that devastates Caribbean reefs. The meter shows "Controlled" instead of "Convinced" because we're controlling a species, not convincing a person. Each player action triggers an ecological counter-response from the lionfish:

🥅 Capture

You try to catch the lionfish with a net for the aquarium.

+12-18 Controlled, -8-14 Hostility
Counter: Flares its venomous spines. Watch out for the sting!
🎣 Fish

You ready the spear. Lionfish is edible and fishing them helps the reef.

+15-22 Controlled, -10-16 Hostility
Counter: Reproduces rapidly. While you catch one, more juveniles appear.
🪸 Protect Coral

You set up barriers to protect the coral and herbivorous fish.

+10-15 Controlled, -6-10 Hostility
Counter: Devours herbivorous fish. Without them, algae invade the coral.
🤿 Alert Divers

You alert other divers to organize a group removal effort.

+18-25 Controlled, -12-18 Hostility
Counter: Hunts juvenile parrotfish. Without them the coral won't be cleaned!

Peaceful resolution: Controlled fishing maintains the reef's balance. Lionfish meat is delicious and nutritious — cooking it is ecological. Lionfish hunting tournaments are a real strategy used in the Dominican Caribbean.

⚙️ Game Mechanics

Inventory

20-slot backpack (5x4) with unique icons drawn by code. Each item has a name, translated description, and type (tool, consumable, key, treasure). Navigate with arrows, use with E, close with I.

Dialogues

Dialogue system with typewriter effect, character name in gold, and support for response options. Dialogues block movement and are advanced with E. Each text is translated into Spanish, French, and English.

Auto-Save

The game saves automatically to localStorage every time the player returns to the world map. Saved data includes: map progress (completed/unlocked nodes), full inventory, active companions, health, character gender, and language. "Continue Game" in the main menu restores all state and takes the player to the world map.

Toasts

Non-intrusive floating messages that appear when picking up items or completing missions. Entrance animation from above, dark background with gold border, maximum 4 simultaneous.

Detection with Magnoboot

Radar pulse (F key) that expands concentric circles. If the pulse touches a buried excavation zone, it reveals it. Then you can dig with E to collect the hidden object.

Risk Map

Semi-transparent overlay with colored zones: green (protected), yellow (at risk), red (threatened). Dotted borders to distinguish heritage risk levels.

Design Principles

  • Readability first: code commented in Spanish, readable by 13-year-old students
  • No heavy dependencies: only LeafletJS for maps, everything else is vanilla
  • Procedural sound: effects are generated with Web Audio API, no files
  • Programmatic sprites: all characters and buildings are drawn with Canvas 2D
  • 3 languages: Spanish, French, and English — every visible string is translated
